I am a PhD candidate in Finance at the David Eccles School of Business, University of Utah, and I am on the 2026–27 academic job market.

My research studies mutual funds and ETFs: where fund-manager skill comes from, and how the migration of active management from mutual funds into ETFs changes investor behavior and manager incentives. I also work on urban economics and municipal finance.

Job Market Paper

Education and Investment Skill: Evidence from Mutual Fund Managers

Job Market Paper

Does the quality of a fund manager’s education predict investment skill? Using syllabi from 669 managers’ colleges, I measure Relevance, fit with investment-management work, and Timeliness, how current coursework is. A one-standard-deviation increase in either predicts 0.5 percentage points higher annual net returns. Read the abstract
